# Contributing

Anyone can open a pull request to add or fix a listing. The project's own team does not have to be the one who submits it, but the first maintainer handle should belong to someone who can speak for the product.

What we accept:

- One listing per product. A product on several chains goes in `shared/` once, not in each chain folder.
- A description taken from the project's own material, in plain words. No rankings, no "best" or "leading", no price talk.
- Links that go to the product and its official channels. No referral or affiliate links, no shortened URLs.
- A logo and cover from the project's own site or brand kit.

We may decline or remove a listing for a product that has been compromised, abandoned or shut down, or one whose links keep failing the weekly check. A shut-down product can stay as `sunset` if its history is useful to readers.

Changes to an existing listing need an approval from one of its `maintainers` or from a repo maintainer.

A listing is not an endorsement, and listing here does not mean Kynraze has reviewed the product's code or security.
